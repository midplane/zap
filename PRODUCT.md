# Zap

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Stack

SwiftUI/AppKit on macOS, Kotlin/Jetpack Compose on Android, TypeScript on Cloudflare Workers. Native stacks explicitly chosen for polish and OS integration.

## Users

People who use a Mac and Android phone and want private clipboard history on their own infrastructure.

## Product Purpose

Capture on Mac, share into Android, and find text or images on either device. A shortcut-driven Mac history panel and a touch-native Android companion share end-to-end encrypted history.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Ten days of history by default, configurable from 1 to 365. Text and static images. Local-first operation. Cloudflare deployment by CLI and Android pairing by QR. History-first sync never overwrites the clipboard on arrival. No custom handling of sensitive clipboard content. No pins, OCR, accounts, public links, or analytics. Apps and backend ship together.

## Product Principles

- Remarkable polish, no frills.
- Simple code and lean behavioral tests.
- Platform-native interactions, consistent visual character.
- Content stays encrypted outside paired devices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operation and screen-reader labels, light/dark appearance, scalable Android text, and reduced-motion support.
